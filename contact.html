FAQ

Frequently Asked Question

1. Can I customize my neon sign?

Absolutely! SigNeon360 offers complete customization options, including size, color, font, and design. You can bring your vision to life with our easy design tools or consult our team for personalized assistance.

2. Are your neon signs safe for indoor use?

Yes, our neon signs are crafted with LED technology, making them safe, energy-efficient, and cool to the touch. They are perfect for homes, offices, and any indoor spaces.

3. How long does it take to receive my order?

Production typically takes 5-7 business days, and shipping times vary depending on your location. We offer worldwide delivery and provide tracking details once your order is shipped.

4. What materials are used in your neon signs?

Our signs are made with premium acrylic backing and LED neon tubing, ensuring durability, lightweight design, and a brilliant glow that lasts for years.

5. Can I use my neon sign outdoors?

Yes, we offer weather-resistant options for outdoor use. Please specify your requirements when placing your order to ensure your sign is suitable for exterior conditions.

6. What if my neon sign arrives damaged?

We prioritize secure packaging, but if your sign arrives damaged, contact us immediately. Our team will arrange for a replacement or repair under our satisfaction guarantee policy.

7. How energy-efficient are your neon signs?

Our signs use LED technology, consuming up to 80% less energy compared to traditional neon signs. They're both cost-effective and environmentally friendly.

8. Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, all our neon signs come with a one-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. If you experience any issues, our support team is here to help. Still have questions? Contact us at any time — we're here to assist you!
